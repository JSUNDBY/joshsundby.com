About

Minneapolis-based immersive artist and projection designer

Josh Sundby creates immersive art installations, generative projection content, and multi-sensory experiences for venues, events, and public spaces across Minneapolis. With 25 years of mindfulness practice, ambient music production, and digital art, he brings a unique perspective to immersive experiences, blending technology with intention to transform how people experience a room. His sound bath and projection work has been featured at venues throughout the Twin Cities.

Capabilities

What I Do

Projection Design

Multi-projector installations with edge blending, mapping, and site-specific content. From intimate rooms to full venue takeovers.

Generative Content

Original visual content that evolves in real time. Cloudscapes, nebulae, abstract geometry, botanical, and custom themes for any environment.

Immersive Events

Sound baths, live performances, wellness experiences, and celebrations. Full production from concept through execution.

Live Visuals

Real-time VJ performance for concerts, DJ sets, and live events. Reactive visuals synced to music and energy.

Digital Art

Sacred geometry, generative form, and bold abstraction. Original works available as prints and commissions.

Venue Consulting

Equipment spec, projector placement, content strategy, and ongoing creative direction for permanent installations.
